How verification is decided
  • Authenticity: The account represents a real person, creator, organization, or legitimate identity.
  • Presence: The creator has an established presence and contributes original content.
  • Consistency: Activity and participation show that the account is genuinely being used.
  • Reputation: No serious pattern of scams, impersonation, abuse, or deceptive behavior.
  • Significance: The creator has meaningful recognition either within TikNation or beyond it.
  • No follower requirement: Follower count alone does not decide verification.
