💚 The fairness rules we are designing around

Host stays in control

Hosts approve guests, can remove them, and can end a battle. Guests should know when their camera and microphone are live.

Transparent gift value

Show the purchase price and creator earnings policy before real purchases. Demo points are not money and do not imply payout.

No pay-to-win surprises

Publish battle scoring rules, disclose whether gifts affect scores, and protect against fraudulent or reversed payments.

Safety for everyone

Provide reporting, blocking, moderation tools, consent prompts, age-appropriate limits, and anti-harassment rules.
