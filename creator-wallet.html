🏦 Cash-out rules we intend to follow

  • Show the exact withdrawable amount before a payout request.
  • Explain verification, processing times, fees, and any minimum in plain language.
  • Never promise a payout before payment-provider confirmation.
  • Provide a transaction history and a way to question a payout.
  • Protect accounts against fraud and unauthorized transactions.
